ud7.1:5.1 | P: “Uddhaṁ adho sabbadhi vippamutto, | S: “Above, below, everywhere free, | T: Above, below, everywhere released,
ud7.1:5.2 | P: Ayaṁhamasmīti anānupassī; | S: not contemplating ‘I am this’. | T: he doesn’t focus on “I am this.”
ud7.1:5.3 | P: Evaṁ vimutto udatāri oghaṁ, | S: Freed like this, he has crossed the flood | T: Thus released, he crosses the flood
ud7.1:5.4 | P: Atiṇṇapubbaṁ apunabbhavāyā”ti. | S: not crossed before, so as to not be reborn.” | T: not crossed before, for the sake of no further becoming.
