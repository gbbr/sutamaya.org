sn45.90:1.1 | P: “Yathayidaṁ, bhikkhave, yonisomanasikārasampadā. | S: “… accomplishment in rational application of mind. | T: accomplishment in careful attention …
sn45.90:1.2 | P: Yonisomanasikārasampannassetaṁ, bhikkhave, bhikkhuno pāṭikaṅkhaṁ—ariyaṁ aṭṭhaṅgikaṁ maggaṁ bhāvessati, ariyaṁ aṭṭhaṅgikaṁ maggaṁ bahulīkarissati. | S: A mendicant accomplished in rational application of mind can expect to develop and cultivate the noble eightfold path. | T: (none)
sn45.90:1.3 | P: Kathañca, bhikkhave, bhikkhu yonisomanasikārasampanno ariyaṁ aṭṭhaṅgikaṁ maggaṁ bhāveti, ariyaṁ aṭṭhaṅgikaṁ maggaṁ bahulīkaroti? | S: And how does a mendicant accomplished in rational application of mind develop and cultivate the noble eightfold path? | T: (none)
sn45.90:1.4 | P: Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu sammādiṭṭhiṁ bhāveti rāgavinayapariyosānaṁ dosavinayapariyosānaṁ mohavinayapariyosānaṁ …pe… sammāsamādhiṁ bhāveti rāgavinayapariyosānaṁ dosavinayapariyosānaṁ mohavinayapariyosānaṁ. | S: It’s when a mendicant develops right view, right purpose, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, and right immersion, which culminate in the removal of greed, hate, and delusion. | T: complete as in §84 … He develops right concentration, which has as its final goal the removal of lust, the removal of hatred, the removal of delusion.
sn45.90:1.5 | P: Evaṁ kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhu yonisomanasikārasampanno ariyaṁ aṭṭhaṅgikaṁ maggaṁ bhāveti, ariyaṁ aṭṭhaṅgikaṁ maggaṁ bahulīkarotī”ti. | S: That’s how a mendicant accomplished in rational application of mind develops and cultivates the noble eightfold path.” | T: It is in this way, bhikkhus, that a bhikkhu who is accomplished in careful attention develops and cultivates the Noble Eightfold Path.”
