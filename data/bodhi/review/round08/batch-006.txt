mn140:19.1 | S: What remains is sheer consciousness, pure and bright. | T: “Then there remains only consciousness, purified and bright.
mn140:19.2 | S: And what does that consciousness know? | T: What does one cognize with that consciousness?
mn140:19.3 | S: It cognizes ‘pleasure’ and ‘pain’ and ‘neutral’. | T: One cognizes: ‘This is pleasant’; one cognizes: ‘This is painful’; one cognizes: ‘This is neither-painful-nor-pleasant.’
mn140:19.4 | S: Pleasant feeling arises dependent on a contact to be experienced as pleasant. | T: In dependence on a contact to be felt as pleasant there arises a pleasant feeling.
mn140:19.5 | S: When they feel a pleasant feeling, they know: ‘I feel a pleasant feeling.’ | T: When one feels a pleasant feeling, one understands: ‘I feel a pleasant feeling.’
mn140:19.6 | S: They know: ‘With the cessation of that contact to be experienced as pleasant, the corresponding pleasant feeling ceases and stops.’ | T: One understands: ‘With the cessation of that same contact to be felt as pleasant, its corresponding feeling—the pleasant feeling that arose in dependence on that contact to be felt as pleasant—ceases and subsides.’
mn140:19.7 | S: Painful feeling arises dependent on a contact to be experienced as painful. | T: In dependence on a contact to be felt as painful there arises a painful feeling.
mn140:19.8 | S: When they feel a painful feeling, they know: ‘I feel a painful feeling.’ | T: When one feels a painful feeling, one understands: ‘I feel a painful feeling.’
mn140:19.9 | S: They know: ‘With the cessation of that contact to be experienced as painful, the corresponding painful feeling ceases and stops.’ | T: One understands: ‘With the cessation of that same contact to be felt as painful, its corresponding feeling—the painful feeling that arose in dependence on that contact to be felt as painful—ceases and subsides.’
mn140:19.10 | S: Neutral feeling arises dependent on a contact to be experienced as neutral. | T: In dependence on a contact to be felt as neither-painful-nor-pleasant there arises a neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling.
mn140:19.11 | S: When they feel a neutral feeling, they know: ‘I feel a neutral feeling.’ | T: When one feels a neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling, one understands: ‘I feel a neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling.’
mn140:19.12 | S: They know: ‘With the cessation of that contact to be experienced as neutral, the corresponding neutral feeling ceases and stops.’ | T: One understands: ‘With the cessation of that same contact to be felt as neither-painful-nor-pleasant, its corresponding feeling—the neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling that arose in dependence on that contact to be felt as neither-painful-nor-pleasant—ceases and subsides.’
