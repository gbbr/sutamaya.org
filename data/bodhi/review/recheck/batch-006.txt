S2: should be regarded as being like that feeble person.
T: So too, [1] when the Dhamma is being taught to someone for the cessation of personality, [2] if his mind does not enter into it and acquire confidence, [3] steadiness, [4] and resolution, [5*] then he can be regarded as like the feeble man. ¶
