sn45.48:1.5 | P: Sace pana vo, bhikkhave, aññatitthiyā paribbājakā evaṁ puccheyyuṁ: | S: If wanderers of other religions were to ask you: | T: “Then, bhikkhus, if the wanderers of other sects ask you:
sn45.48:1.6 | P: ‘atthi panāvuso, maggo, atthi paṭipadā anupādāparinibbānāyā’ti, evaṁ puṭṭhā tumhe, bhikkhave, tesaṁ aññatitthiyānaṁ paribbājakānaṁ evaṁ byākareyyātha: | S: ‘Is there a path and a practice for complete extinguishment with no fuel for grasping?’ You should answer them like this: | T: ‘But, friends, is there a path, is there a way for attaining final Nibbāna without clinging?’—being asked thus, you should answer them thus:
sn45.48:1.7 | P: ‘atthi kho, āvuso, maggo, atthi paṭipadā anupādāparinibbānāyā’ti. | S: ‘There is a path and a practice for complete extinguishment with no fuel for grasping.’ | T: ‘There is a path, friends, there is a way for attaining final Nibbāna without clinging.’

sn45.48:1.9 | P: Ayameva ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo, seyyathidaṁ— | S: It is simply this noble eightfold path, that is: | T: It is this Noble Eightfold Path; that is,

sn45.48:1.11 | P: Ayaṁ, bhikkhave, maggo, ayaṁ paṭipadā anupādāparinibbānāyāti. | S: This is the path, this is the practice for complete extinguishment with no fuel for grasping. | T: This is the path, this is the way for attaining final Nibbāna without clinging.
sn45.48:1.12 | P: Evaṁ puṭṭhā tumhe, bhikkhave, tesaṁ aññatitthiyānaṁ paribbājakānaṁ evaṁ byākareyyāthā”ti. | S: When questioned by wanderers of other religions, that’s how you should answer them.” | T: “Being asked thus, bhikkhus, you should answer those wanderers of other sects in such a way.”
