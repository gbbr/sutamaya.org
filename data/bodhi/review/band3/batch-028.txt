S1: This should be truly seen with right understanding like this: ‘This is not mine, I am not this, this is not my self.’
S2: When you truly see with right understanding, you grow disillusioned with the earth element, detaching the mind from the earth element.
T: And that should be seen as it actually is with proper wisdom thus: [1] ‘This is not mine, [2] this I am not, [3] this is not my self.’ [4*] When one sees it thus as it actually is with proper wisdom, [5] one becomes disenchanted with the earth element and makes the mind dispassionate toward the earth element. ¶
