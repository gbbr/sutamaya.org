sn22.26:2.4 | S: Knowledge and vision arose in me: | T: “The knowledge and vision arose in me:
sn22.26:2.5 | S: ‘My freedom is unshakable; this is my last rebirth; now there’ll be no more future lives.’” | T: ‘Unshakable is my liberation of mind; this is my last birth; now there is no more renewed existence.’”
