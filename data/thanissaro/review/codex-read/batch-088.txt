ud5.8:1.2 | S: At one time the Buddha was staying near Rājagaha in the Bamboo Grove, the squirrels’ feeding ground. | T: on one occasion the Blessed One was staying near Rājagaha at the Bamboo Forest, the Squirrels’ refuge.
ud5.8:1.3 | S: Now at that time Venerable Ānanda robed up in the morning and, taking his bowl and robe, entered Rājagaha for alms. | T: And on that occasion, early in the morning of the uposatha, Ven. Ānanda adjusted his under robe and–carrying his bowl & robes–went into Rājagaha for alms.
ud5.8:2.1 | S: Devadatta saw him wandering for alms, | T: Devadatta saw Ven. Ānanda going for alms in Rājagaha and,
ud5.8:2.2 | S: so he went up to him and said, | T: on seeing him, went to him. On arrival, he said to him,
ud5.8:2.3 | S: “From this day forth, Reverend Ānanda, I shall perform the sabbath and legal proceedings of the Saṅgha apart from the Buddha and the Saṅgha of mendicants.” | T: “From this day forward, friend Ānanda, I will conduct the uposatha & community transactions apart from the Blessed One, apart from the community of monks.”
ud5.8:3.1 | S: Then Ānanda wandered for alms in Rājagaha. After the meal, on his return from almsround, he went to the Buddha, bowed, sat down to one side, | T: Then Ven. Ānanda–having gone for alms in Rājagaha, after the meal, returning from his alms round–went to the Blessed One and, on arrival, having bowed down to him, sat to one side.
ud5.8:3.2 | S: and told him what had just happened, adding: | T: As he was sitting there he said to the Blessed One,
ud5.8:4.1 | P: “Idhāhaṁ, bhante, pubbaṇhasamayaṁ nivāsetvā pattacīvaramādāya rājagahaṁ piṇḍāya pāvisiṁ. | T: “Just now, lord, after adjusting my under robe early in the morning and carrying my bowl & robes, I went into Rājagaha for alms.
ud5.8:4.2 | P: Addasā kho maṁ, bhante, devadatto rājagahe piṇḍāya carantaṁ. | T: Devadatta saw me going for alms in Rājagaha and,
ud5.8:4.3 | P: Disvāna yenāhaṁ tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā maṁ etadavoca: | T: on seeing me, went up to me. On arrival, he said to me,
ud5.8:4.4 | P: ‘ajjatagge dānāhaṁ, āvuso ānanda, aññatreva bhagavatā aññatra bhikkhusaṅghā uposathaṁ karissāmi saṅghakammāni cā’ti. | T: ‘From this day forward, friend Ānanda, I will conduct the uposatha & community transactions apart from the Blessed One, apart from the community of monks.’
ud5.8:4.5 | S: “Today, sir, Devadatta will split the Saṅgha. He will perform the sabbath and legal proceedings of the Saṅgha.” | T: Lord, today Devadatta will split the community. He will conduct the uposatha & community transactions [apart from the community].”
ud5.8:5.1 | S: Then, understanding this matter, on that occasion the Buddha expressed this heartfelt sentiment: | T: Then, on realizing the significance of that, the Blessed One on that occasion exclaimed:
ud5.8:6.1 | S: “It’s easy for the good to do good, | T: The good, for the good, is easy to do.
ud5.8:6.2 | S: but for the bad, doing good is hard. | T: The good, for the evil, is hard to do.
ud5.8:6.3 | S: For the bad, it’s easy to do bad, | T: Evil, for the evil, is easy to do.
ud5.8:6.4 | S: but for the noble ones, doing bad is hard.” | T: Evil, for the noble ones, is hard to do.
ud5.8:6.5 | P: Aṭṭhamaṁ. | T: (none)
