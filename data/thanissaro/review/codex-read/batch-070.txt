These are called dark deeds with dark results. | T: There is the case where a certain person fabricates an injurious bodily fabrication, fabricates an injurious verbal fabrication, fabricates an injurious mental fabrication. an4.237:3.1 | S: And what are bright deeds with bright results? | T: “And what is kamma that is bright with bright result?

This is called kamma that is dark & bright with dark & bright result.
an4.237:5.1 | S: And what are neither dark nor bright deeds with neither dark nor bright results, which lead to the ending of deeds? | T: “And what is kamma that is neither dark nor bright with neither dark nor bright result, leading to the ending of kamma?

an4.237:5.3 | S: These are called neither dark nor bright deeds with neither dark nor bright results, which lead to the ending of deeds. | T: This is called kamma that is neither dark nor bright with neither dark nor bright result, leading to the ending of kamma.
an4.237:5.4 | S: These are the four kinds of deeds that I declare, having realized them with my own insight.” | T: “These, monks, are the four types of kamma directly known, verified, & made known by me.”
