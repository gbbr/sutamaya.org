an5.255:1.1 | P: “Pañcannaṁ, bhikkhave, macchariyānaṁ pahānāya samucchedāya brahmacariyaṁ vussati. | S: “Mendicants, the spiritual life is lived to give up and cut out these five kinds of stinginess. | T: “With the abandoning and eradication of the five forms of stinginess, the holy life is fulfilled.
