an5.256:1.1 | P: “Pañcime, bhikkhave, dhamme appahāya abhabbo paṭhamaṁ jhānaṁ upasampajja viharituṁ. | S: “Mendicants, without giving up these five qualities you can’t enter and remain in the first absorption. | T: “Without abandoning these five qualities, one is incapable of entering & remaining in the first jhāna.
an5.256:1.2 | P: Katame pañca? | S: What five? | T: Which five?
an5.256:1.3 | P: Āvāsamacchariyaṁ, kulamacchariyaṁ, lābhamacchariyaṁ, vaṇṇamacchariyaṁ, dhammamacchariyaṁ. | S: Stinginess with dwellings, families, material things, praise, and the teachings. | T: Stinginess as to one’s monastery (lodgings), stinginess as to one’s family (of supporters), stinginess as to one’s gains, stinginess as to one’s status, and stinginess as to the Dhamma.
an5.256:1.4 | P: Ime kho, bhikkhave, pañca dhamme appahāya abhabbo paṭhamaṁ jhānaṁ upasampajja viharituṁ. | S: Without giving up these five qualities you can’t enter and remain in the first absorption. | T: Without abandoning these five qualities, one is incapable of entering & remaining in the first jhāna.
an5.256:2.1 | P: Pañcime, bhikkhave, dhamme pahāya bhabbo paṭhamaṁ jhānaṁ upasampajja viharituṁ. | S: But after giving up these five qualities you can enter and remain in the first absorption. | T: “With the abandoning of these five qualities, one is capable of entering & remaining in the first jhāna.
an5.256:2.2 | P: Katame pañca? | S: What five? | T: Which five?
an5.256:2.3 | P: Āvāsamacchariyaṁ, kulamacchariyaṁ, lābhamacchariyaṁ, vaṇṇamacchariyaṁ, dhammamacchariyaṁ. | S: Stinginess with dwellings, families, material things, praise, and the teachings. | T: Stinginess as to one’s monastery (lodgings)… one’s family (of supporters)… one’s gains… one’s status, and stinginess as to the Dhamma.
an5.256:2.4 | P: Ime kho, bhikkhave, pañca dhamme pahāya bhabbo paṭhamaṁ jhānaṁ upasampajja viharitun”ti. | S: After giving up these five qualities you can enter and remain in the first absorption.” | T: With the abandoning of these five qualities, one is capable of entering & remaining in the first jhāna.”
