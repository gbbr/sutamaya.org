# Sixth audit of Ṭhānissaro’s segmenting

This audit reads fresh SN, AN and KN texts after the fourteen open verse places were judged
and the applicable repairs verified and copied back. Every selected row was read, including
Pali-only rows. Original Pali appears on every row; Sujato’s English is secondary context.

## The draw

`draw.py`, with seed `20261006`, draws whole texts as audit5 did, with roughly equal line budgets
across SN, AN and KN and a target of about 2,500 rows. It excludes all 157 distinct texts drawn by
audits 1–5, including the original unnumbered `audit/` folder. Texts are shuffled uniformly within
each collection: this is a stratified whole-text sample, not a uniform sample of individual rows.
`draw.json` records the 100 selected texts and counts; `exclusions.json` records the exclusions.

The original `batch-NNN.txt` packets were generated by `review-rounds.py thanissaro read --every`.
The corresponding `.pali.txt` files add Pali to every row and preserve current English line breaks
as `↵`; original source HTML remains the authority for whole source lines. Three independent
Codex readers at effort high read all seven batches. A separate judge at effort xhigh read no
audit6 batches and checked every proposal against the original Pali and source HTML.
Reader assignments: A batches 001/004/007 (915 rows); B 002/005 (841); C 003/006 (761).
Each reader’s `.findings` ends with `# done`; per-text coverage and rationales are in `.reader.json`.
`coverage.json` verifies all 2,517 rows and every batch; saved inputs were unchanged while reading.

## The errors, by kind

- AN 10.96:10.1 starts the separately rendered non-eternal-cosmos exchange, formerly appended to
  the preceding eternal-cosmos exchange on 9.1.
- AN 10.96:12.3 starts the affirmative knowing/seeing answer; the preceding negative construction
  belongs to 12.2.
- AN 4.192:9.9 empties into the compressed sentence beginning at 9.5. Its Pali repeats the
  predicate already rendered there after omitted explanation. The source abbreviates that
  explanation with an ellipsis. This repeated/abbreviated Pali stays hidden; no coverage is added.

Sound clause/formula cuts, source omissions, source wordplay and verse lines ordered differently
from their Pali were left alone, following the app/review rules. Reader concern records preserve
these distinctions separately from accepted errors.

## Application and verification

The kept repairs are in `accepted.findings` and `../hand/audit6.findings`. Historical directives
for those keys are explicitly superseded in `superseded.json`. Application is in the disposable
copy `/private/tmp/sutamaya-thanissaro-audit6`. All three directives passed in three trial groups
and changed six English rows in two texts. `check-upstream.py` reports 0 differ and
`check-integrity.py` 0 problems. `check-round.py` reports 0 partial source verse lines and
0 hidden translated verse Pali; its only newly hidden row, AN 4.192:9.9, is approved abbreviated
repetition as recorded in `round-judgment.json`, and receives no coverage. The final cross/list
rounds found no suspects. A plain segmenter rerun in the fresh copy
`/private/tmp/sutamaya-thanissaro-audit6-regeneration` reproduced all 2,679 corpus, cut and coverage
files exactly. Verified results were copied back to `translations`, and copy-back hashes match.
Logs, `trial.json`, `changed-texts.json`, `results.json` and regeneration hashes preserve the
evidence. No commits are authorized or made.

## Verdict

The measured overall rate is 0.12%, compared with audit5’s 58/2,504 = 2.32%. Each sampled collection
is below the approximate 0.5% benchmark. The sample and settled verse rules support a lower
measured boundary rate; this sample does not prove the remaining corpus free of boundary errors.
Three targeted Job 1 cases still need segmenter/checker support: SN 4.24’s cross-text prose,
AN 4.19’s reordered prose, and Ud 7.8’s editorial commentary. Their exact required moves are in
`../verse-open/deferred.json`.

The subsequent Claude review from another model is pending; this report does not claim it done.
