S2: (Pali) Pañcime, bhikkhave, dhamme pahāya bhabbo dutiyaṁ jhānaṁ … tatiyaṁ jhānaṁ … catutthaṁ jhānaṁ … sotāpattiphalaṁ … sakadāgāmiphalaṁ … anāgāmiphalaṁ … arahattaṁ sacchikātuṁ. | But after giving up these five qualities you can enter and remain in the second absorption … third absorption … fourth absorption … and realize the fruit of stream-entry … the fruit of once-return … the fruit of non-return … perfection.
T: Without abandoning these five qualities, [1*] one is incapable of entering & remaining in the second jhāna… [2] the third jhāna… [3] the fourth jhāna; [4] one is incapable realizing the fruit of stream-entry… [5] the fruit of once-returning… [6] the fruit of non-returning… [7] arahantship. ¶ [8] “With the abandoning of these five qualities, [9] one is capable of entering & remaining in the second jhāna… [10] the third jhāna… [11] the fourth jhāna; [12] capable of realizing the fruit of stream-entry… [13] the fruit of once-returning… [14] the fruit of non-returning… 

an5.265-271:2.4
S1: (Pali) Pañcime, bhikkhave, dhamme pahāya bhabbo dutiyaṁ jhānaṁ … tatiyaṁ jhānaṁ … catutthaṁ jhānaṁ … sotāpattiphalaṁ … sakadāgāmiphalaṁ … anāgāmiphalaṁ … arahattaṁ sacchikātuṁ. Katame pañca? Āvāsamacchariyaṁ, kulamacchariyaṁ, lābhamacchariyaṁ, vaṇṇamacchariyaṁ, akataññutaṁ akataveditaṁ. | But after giving up these five qualities you can enter and remain in the second absorption … third absorption … fourth absorption … and realize the fruit of stream-entry … the fruit of once-return … the fruit of non-return … perfection. What five? Stinginess with dwellings, families, material things, praise, and lack of gratitude and thankfulness.
S2: (Pali) Ime kho, bhikkhave, pañca dhamme pahāya bhabbo arahattaṁ sacchikātun”ti. | After giving up these five qualities you can realize perfection.”
T: one is incapable of entering & remaining in the second jhāna… [1] the third jhāna… [2] the fourth jhāna; [3] one is incapable realizing the fruit of stream-entry… [4] the fruit of once-returning… [5] the fruit of non-returning… [6] arahantship. ¶ [7] “With the abandoning of these five qualities, [8] one is capable of entering & remaining in the second jhāna… [9] the third jhāna… [10] the fourth jhāna; [11] capable of realizing the fruit of stream-entry… [12] the fruit of once-returning… [13] the fruit of non-returning… [14*] arahantship.…”
