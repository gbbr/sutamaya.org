mn17:26.6 | S: And the necessities of life are easy to come by.’ | T: And the requisites of life that should be obtained by one gone forth—robes, almsfood, lodging, or medicinal requisites used for curing the sick—are obtained without difficulty.’
mn17:26.7 | S: That mendicant should follow that individual for the rest of their life; they should not leave them, even if driven away.” | T: That monk should stay with that individual for life. He shouldn’t leave, even if driven away.”
mn17:26.8 | S: That is what the Buddha said. | T: That is what the Blessed One said.
mn17:26.9 | S: Satisfied, the mendicants approved what the Buddha said. | T: Gratified, the monks delighted in the Blessed One’s words.
