# an4.19
Source: data/upstream/thanissaro/sutta/an/an4/an4.19.html

SOURCE 001 prose: “There are these four ways of going off course. Which four? One goes off course through desire. One goes off course through aversion. One goes off course through delusion. One goes off course through fear. These are the four ways of going off course.”
SOURCE 002 verse: If you—
SOURCE 003 verse: through desire,
SOURCE 004 verse: aversion,
SOURCE 005 verse: delusion,
SOURCE 006 verse: fear—
SOURCE 007 verse: transgress the Dhamma,
SOURCE 008 verse: your honor wanes,
SOURCE 009 verse: as in the dark fortnight,
SOURCE 010 verse: the moon.
SOURCE 011 prose: “There are these four ways of not going off course. Which four? One doesn’t go off course through desire. One doesn’t go off course through aversion. One doesn’t go off course through delusion. One doesn’t go off course through fear. These are the four ways of not going off course.”
SOURCE 012 verse: If you don’t—
SOURCE 013 verse: through desire,
SOURCE 014 verse: aversion,
SOURCE 015 verse: delusion,
SOURCE 016 verse: fear—
SOURCE 017 verse: transgress the Dhamma,
SOURCE 018 verse: your honor waxes,
SOURCE 019 verse: as in the bright fortnight,
SOURCE 020 verse: the moon.
SOURCE 021 prose: See also: AN 9:7
