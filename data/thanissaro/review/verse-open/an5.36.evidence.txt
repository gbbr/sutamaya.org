# an5.36
Source: data/upstream/thanissaro/sutta/an/an5/an5.36.html

SOURCE 001 prose: “There are these five seasonable gifts. Which five? One gives to a newcomer. One gives to one going away. One gives to one who is ill. One gives in time of famine. One sets the first fruits of field & orchard in front of those who are virtuous. These are the five seasonable gifts.”
SOURCE 002 verse: In the proper season they give—
SOURCE 003 verse: those with discernment,
SOURCE 004 verse: responsive, free from stinginess.
SOURCE 005 verse: Having been given in proper season,
SOURCE 006 verse: with hearts inspired by the noble ones
SOURCE 007 verse: —straightened, Such—
SOURCE 008 verse: their offering bears an abundance.
SOURCE 009 verse: Those who rejoice in that gift
SOURCE 010 verse: or give assistance,
SOURCE 011 verse: they, too, have a share of the merit,
SOURCE 012 verse: and the offering isn’t depleted by that.
SOURCE 013 verse: So, with an unhesitant mind,
SOURCE 014 verse: one should give where the gift bears great fruit.
SOURCE 015 verse: Merit is what establishes
SOURCE 016 verse: living beings in the next life.
