# sn4.24
Source: data/upstream/thanissaro/sutta/sn/sn4/sn4.24.html

SOURCE 001 prose: I have heard that on one occasion, when the Blessed One was newly self-awakened, he was dwelling near Uruvelā on the bank of the Nerañjarā River, at the root of the Goatherd’s Banyan Tree.
SOURCE 002 prose: And at that time, Māra the Evil One had been following the Blessed One for seven years, looking for an opening, but unable to find one.
SOURCE 003 prose: Then Māra the Evil One went to the Blessed One and addressed him in verse:
SOURCE 004 verse: “Practicing jhāna in the forest—
SOURCE 005 verse: are you overcome with grief?
SOURCE 006 verse: Have you lost,
SOURCE 007 verse: or do you desire, wealth?
SOURCE 008 verse: Have you done something
SOURCE 009 verse: blameworthy in the village?
SOURCE 010 verse: Is it because
SOURCE 011 verse: you become intimate with people,
SOURCE 012 verse: but intimacy doesn’t prosper for you
SOURCE 013 verse: with anyone at all?”
SOURCE 014 verse: The Buddha:
SOURCE 015 verse: “Having dug up all the root of grief,
SOURCE 016 verse: blameless, I practice jhāna not grieving.
SOURCE 017 verse: Defeating all greed, all
SOURCE 018 verse: yearning for becoming,
SOURCE 019 verse: I, without effluent, practice jhāna—
SOURCE 020 verse: you, Kinsman of the Heedless.”
SOURCE 021 verse: Māra:
SOURCE 022 verse: “Of whatever they say,
SOURCE 023 verse: ‘This is mine,’
SOURCE 024 verse: and whoever says, ‘mine,’
SOURCE 025 verse: if your heart is here,
SOURCE 026 verse: contemplative, you’re not free from me.”
SOURCE 027 verse: The Buddha:
SOURCE 028 verse: “Of whatever they speak,
SOURCE 029 verse: it’s not mine.
SOURCE 030 verse: And whoever speaks
SOURCE 031 verse: they are not me.
SOURCE 032 verse: Know that, Evil One.
SOURCE 033 verse: You can’t even see my tracks.”
SOURCE 034 verse: Māra:
SOURCE 035 verse: “If the path has been awakened to—
SOURCE 036 verse: secure, leading to the deathless—
SOURCE 037 verse: then go away! Go alone!
SOURCE 038 verse: What’s the use of instructing others?”
SOURCE 039 verse: The Buddha:
SOURCE 040 verse: “People seeking the far side
SOURCE 041 verse: ask about the deathless realm.
SOURCE 042 verse: Asked by them, I explain
SOURCE 043 verse: the truth that’s acquisition-free.”
SOURCE 044 verse: Māra:
SOURCE 045 prose: “Suppose, lord, that not far from a village or town was a pond. There in it was a crab. Then a number of boys & girls, leaving the village or town, would go to the pond and, on arrival, would take the crab out of the water and place it on the ground. And whenever the crab extended a leg, the boys or girls would cut it off, break it, and smash it with sticks or stones right there, so that the crab—with all its legs cut off, broken, & smashed—would be unable to get back in the pond as before.
SOURCE 046 prose: “In the same way, whatever writhings, tricks, & contortions [there are], the Blessed One has cut them off, broken them, and smashed them all. Now I’m unable to approach the Blessed One, looking for an opening.”
SOURCE 047 prose: Then Māra the Evil One recited these verses of dejection in the Blessed One’s presence:
SOURCE 048 verse: “A crow circled a stone
SOURCE 049 verse: the color of fat—
SOURCE 050 verse: ‘Perhaps I’ll find something tender here,
SOURCE 051 verse: Maybe there’s something tasty’
SOURCE 052 verse: —but not getting anything tasty there,
SOURCE 053 verse: the crow went away.
SOURCE 054 verse: Like a crow attacking the rock,
SOURCE 055 verse: I weary myself with Gotama.”
SOURCE 056 prose: Then Māra the Evil One, having recited these verses of dejection in the Blessed One’s presence, left that place and sat on the ground not far from the Blessed One, silent, abashed, with his shoulders drooping, his head down, brooding, at a loss for words, drawing in the dirt with a stick.
SOURCE 057 prose: See also: SN 4:25; Sn 3:2
