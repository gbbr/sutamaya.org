thag12.1:6.2 | P: cittassa abhihāsanaṁ; | S: an enjoyment for the mind; | T: the delight of the mind,
thag12.1:6.3 | P: Titthañca sabbabuddhānaṁ, | S: the ford where all the Buddhas cross over: | T: the ford of all Buddhas:
thag12.1:6.4 | P: tasmā sīlaṁ visodhaye. | S: that’s why you should purify your ethics. | T: Therefore virtue should be purified.

## thag12.1:7.1–thag12.1:7.4 (thag)
thag12.1:7.1 | P: Sīlaṁ balaṁ appaṭimaṁ, | S: Ethics are the matchless power; | T: Virtue: an incomparable strength.
thag12.1:7.2 | P: sīlaṁ āvudhamuttamaṁ; | S: ethics are the ultimate weapon; | T: Virtue: the ultimate weapon.
thag12.1:7.3 | P: Sīlamābharaṇaṁ seṭṭhaṁ, | S: ethics are the best ornament; | T: Virtue: the highest adornment.
thag12.1:7.4 | P: sīlaṁ kavacamabbhutaṁ. | S: ethics are a marvelous coat of armor. | T: Virtue: an astounding coat of mail.

## thag12.1:8.1–thag12.1:8.4 (thag)
thag12.1:8.1 | P: Sīlaṁ setu mahesakkho, | S: Ethics are a mighty bridge; | T: Virtue: an imposing bridge.
thag12.1:8.2 | P: sīlaṁ gandho anuttaro; | S: ethics are the unsurpassed scent; | T: Virtue: an unexcelled fragrance.
thag12.1:8.3 | P: Sīlaṁ vilepanaṁ seṭṭhaṁ, | S: ethics are the best perfume, | T: Virtue: the highest fragrant ointment
thag12.1:8.4 | P: yena vāti disodisaṁ. | S: that float from place to place. | T: by which one wafts ⏎ this direction & that.

## thag12.1:9.1–thag12.1:9.4 (thag)
thag12.1:9.1 | P: Sīlaṁ sambalamevaggaṁ, | S: Ethics are the best provision; | T: Virtue: the supreme provision.
thag12.1:9.2 | P: sīlaṁ pātheyyamuttamaṁ; | S: ethics are the unsurpassed supply for a journey; | T: Virtue: the ultimate food for the journey.
thag12.1:9.3 | P: Sīlaṁ seṭṭho ativāho, | S: ethics are the best vehicle | T: Virtue: the highest vehicle
thag12.1:9.4 | P: yena yāti disodisaṁ. | S: that take you from place to place. | T: by which one goes ⏎ this direction & that.
