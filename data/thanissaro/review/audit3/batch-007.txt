## sn12.52
sn12.52:1.1 | S: At Sāvatthī. | T: Near Sāvatthī.
sn12.52:1.2 | S: “There are things that fuel grasping. When you concentrate on the gratification provided by these things, your craving grows. | T: There the Blessed One said to the monks: “In one who keeps focusing on the allure of clingable phenomena [or: phenomena that offer sustenance = the five aggregates], craving develops.
sn12.52:1.3 | S: Craving is a requirement for grasping. | T: From craving as a requisite condition comes clinging/sustenance.
sn12.52:1.4 | S: Grasping is a requirement for continued existence. | T: From clinging/sustenance as a requisite condition comes becoming.
sn12.52:1.5 | S: Continued existence is a requirement for rebirth. | T: From becoming as a requisite condition comes birth.
sn12.52:1.6 | S: Rebirth is a requirement for old age and death, sorrow, lamentation, pain, sadness, and distress to come to be. | T: From birth as a requisite condition, then aging-&-death, sorrow, lamentation, pain, distress, & despair come into play.
sn12.52:1.7 | S: That is how this entire mass of suffering originates. | T: Such is the origin of this entire mass of suffering & stress.
sn12.52:2.1 | S: Suppose a great mass of fire was burning with ten, twenty, thirty, or forty loads of wood. | T: “Just as if a great mass of fire of ten… twenty… thirty or forty cartloads of timber were burning,
sn12.52:2.2 | S: And from time to time someone would toss in dry grass, cow dung, or wood. | T: and into it a man would time & again throw dried grass, dried cow dung, & dried timber,
sn12.52:2.3 | S: Fed and fuelled by that, the great mass of fire would burn for a long time. | T: so that the great mass of fire—thus nourished, thus sustained—would burn for a long, long time.
sn12.52:2.4 | S: In the same way, there are things that fuel grasping. When you concentrate on the gratification provided by these things, your craving grows. | T: In the same way, in one who keeps focusing on the allure of clingable phenomena, craving develops.
sn12.52:2.5 | S: Craving is a requirement for grasping. … | T: From craving as a requisite condition comes clinging/sustenance. From clinging/sustenance as a requisite condition comes becoming. From becoming as a requisite condition comes birth. From birth as a requisite condition, then aging-&-death, sorrow, lamentation, pain, distress, & despair come into play.
sn12.52:2.6 | S: That is how this entire mass of suffering originates. | T: Such is the origin of this entire mass of suffering & stress.
sn12.52:3.1 | S: There are things that fuel grasping. When you concentrate on the drawbacks of these things, your craving ceases. | T: “Now, in one who keeps focusing on the drawbacks of clingable phenomena, craving ceases.
sn12.52:3.2 | S: When craving ceases, grasping ceases. | T: From the cessation of craving comes the cessation of clinging/sustenance.
sn12.52:3.3 | S: When grasping ceases, continued existence ceases. | T: From the cessation of clinging/sustenance comes the cessation of becoming.
sn12.52:3.4 | S: When continued existence ceases, rebirth ceases. | T: From the cessation of becoming comes the cessation of birth.
sn12.52:3.5 | S: When rebirth ceases, old age and death, sorrow, lamentation, pain, sadness, and distress cease. | T: From the cessation of birth, then aging, illness & death, sorrow, lamentation, pain, distress, & despair all cease.
sn12.52:3.6 | S: That is how this entire mass of suffering ceases. | T: Such is the cessation of this entire mass of suffering & stress.
sn12.52:4.1 | S: Suppose a great mass of fire was burning with ten, twenty, thirty, or forty loads of wood. | T: “Just as if a great mass of fire of ten… twenty… thirty or forty cartloads of timber were burning,
sn12.52:4.2 | S: And no one would toss in dry grass, cow dung, or wood from time to time. | T: into which a man simply would not time & again throw dried grass, dried cow dung, or dried timber,
sn12.52:4.3 | S: As the original fuel is used up and no more is added, the great mass of fire would be extinguished due to not being fed. | T: so that the great mass of fire—its original sustenance being consumed, and no other being offered—would, without nutriment, go out.
sn12.52:4.4 | S: In the same way, there are things that fuel grasping. When you concentrate on the drawbacks of these things, your craving ceases. | T: In the same way, in one who keeps focusing on the drawbacks of clingable phenomena, craving ceases.
sn12.52:4.5 | S: When craving ceases, grasping ceases. … | T: From the cessation of craving comes the cessation of clinging/sustenance. From the cessation of clinging/sustenance comes the cessation of becoming. From the cessation of becoming comes the cessation of birth. From the cessation of birth, then aging, illness & death, sorrow, lamentation, pain, distress, & despair all cease.
sn12.52:4.6 | S: That is how this entire mass of suffering ceases.” | T: Such is the cessation of this entire mass of suffering & stress.”
sn12.52:4.7 | P: Dutiyaṁ. | T: (none)
