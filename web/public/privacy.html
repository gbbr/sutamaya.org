Privacy

Sutamaya keeps as little about you as it can. You can read the entire canon without an account, and until you sign in nothing you do here leaves your device.

If you don't sign in

Your lists, notes, highlights, reading position and display settings are stored only in your browser, on that device. None of it is sent anywhere, and clearing the site's data in your browser erases it for good.

If you sign in

There are two ways in, and each one takes only what it needs to identify you:

  • Google. Your email address, your name, your profile picture and your Google account identifier. Nothing else — no contacts, no calendar, no files.
  • A six-digit code by email. Your email address, and the code itself, which is stored scrambled and expires shortly after it is sent.

From then on, what you write in the app is stored on the server against your account:

  • your lists and the folders you put them in
  • your notes
  • your highlights — which sutta, which passage, which colour
  • which suttas you have opened, and when
  • a random identifier for each device you sign in on, which is how two devices work out whose version of an edit is the newer one

Signing in also sets one cookie, which identifies your account and nothing else. There are no tracking cookies.

What never happens

There is no advertising here and no tracking of you between sites. Nothing about you is sold, rented or shared. Your notes and highlights are not read, mined or profiled — they are stored so they can be shown back to you and kept in step across your devices, and for nothing else.

What is counted

The app runs Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts how often each page is opened. It sets no cookie, stores nothing on your device, and follows nobody from one site to another: it records that a page was opened, never who opened it, so it cannot say which suttas you in particular have read. It also only runs while you are online, so anything you read offline is counted nowhere at all. Only the app is counted this way; these public pages run no measurement whatsoever.

Who else is involved

  • Cloudflare hosts the site, holds the database, and provides the page counting described above. Like any web host it sees the ordinary details of a request, including your IP address.
  • Google, only if you choose the Google button.
  • Resend delivers the sign-in code, so it sees your email address.

That is the whole list. The walkthrough on the home page is a link to YouTube rather than an embedded player, so YouTube learns nothing unless you follow it.

Getting a copy, and getting rid of it

  • Export. Settings → Export my data downloads everything held against your account as a single file.
  • Delete. Write to metta@sutamaya.org and your account and everything in it will be deleted. There is no button for this yet.
  • Just this device. Settings has an option to erase the copy held locally, which leaves the account untouched.

When you delete a note, a list or a highlight, its contents go and a small marker is kept saying that it was deleted — that marker is what stops a device that was offline at the time from putting it back. Everything else is kept for as long as the account exists.

Children

Sutamaya is not aimed at children, and accounts are not knowingly created for anyone under 13.

Changes, and getting in touch

If any of this changes, this page changes with it and the date below says when. Questions about any of it go to metta@sutamaya.org.
