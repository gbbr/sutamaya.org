Terms

Sutamaya is a personal project, offered as it is. No money changes hands: there is nothing to buy, no subscription, and no plan to add either.

Using it

Read, search, make lists, write notes and mark up passages, on as many devices as you like. Nothing is asked in return.

Your account

The email address you sign in with is yours to look after — anyone who can read that inbox can reach your account. An account that is being used to attack or abuse the service can be suspended or removed.

What you write stays yours

Your lists, notes and highlights belong to you. They are stored so they can be shown back to you and kept in step across your devices, and for no other purpose. You can export them at any time from Settings, and ask for them to be deleted — see the privacy policy.

Where the texts come from

  • The Pali and the English translations are from SuttaCentral, dedicated to the public domain under CC0. Bhikkhu Sujato's English is edited in places — the changes are listed here.
  • The Pali definitions are from the Digital Pali Dictionary by Bodhirasa, under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Its non-commercial terms are one of the reasons nothing here will ever be sold.
  • The app's own code is MIT-licensed and lives on GitHub.

No warranty

Sutamaya is provided as it is, with no warranty of any kind. There is no promise that it will be available, that it will be free of errors, or that what you have written will survive — so if a note matters to you, keep an exported copy of it. To the fullest extent the law allows, no liability is accepted for any loss arising from using it.

None of which is indifference. I read in this app every day, and my own lists and notes sit in the same database as yours, so I will do everything I can to keep it running and to keep what you have written safe. I just can't promise it.

Changes, and stopping

Features may change, and the service may one day be shut down. If it is being shut down, this site will say so with enough notice to export your data first. You may stop using it whenever you like; deleting your account ends these terms.
