A study app for the Pali suttas.

Sutamaya is a reader for the Early Buddhist Texts, built to work offline. Browse the whole canon, keep your own lists, highlight passages, write notes, and tap any Pali word to see what it means.

Watch a 4-minute walkthrough

Works in any browser — phone, tablet or desktop.

The whole canon, easy to move around in

The four main nikāyas and the Khuddaka books, arranged the way they are actually read — collection, chapter, sutta. Search by reference, title or Pali.

Pali beside the English, word by word

Show the Pali line under each segment of translation, and tap any word to open a dictionary definition in a dock at the bottom of the screen — from the Digital Pali Dictionary, without leaving the text you're reading.

Set the page the way you read

Typeface, text size, line height, light or dark. Verse, headings and speakers are set the way the source marks them, so a long text stays readable for as long as you want to stay in it.

Highlights and notes

Select any passage and highlight it in one of three colours. Write a note on a sutta and it stays with it. Tag a sutta with a word of your own and it becomes a way back to it later.

Your own lists

Save any sutta to one or more lists, group your lists into folders, and reorder both by dragging. Recently read, everything you've highlighted, and everything you've made a note on are kept as lists of their own, automatically.

On every device you read on

Sign in and your lists, notes, highlights and reading history are stored in the cloud and follow you to your phone, tablet and laptop. Edits made offline sync the next time that device connects.

Built for reading offline

Every sutta you open is kept on your device, so anything you've read stays available with no connection. One tap in Settings downloads the entire canon and dictionary in advance — on a plane, on retreat, or in airplane mode, the app opens and behaves exactly the same.

Open it and start reading.
