Method: the review manifests define verse key sets; baseline sutta JSON supplies settled answers.
As in the previous report, the answer supplies only the whole verse's contiguous source envelope;
its internal row cuts are withheld from layoutVerse. The measurement uses the patched segmenter's
own parser and layout functions, Sujato/Pali context and the ordinary learned table, with no verse
IDs, cut decisions or review answers passed to inference. Exact means identical normalized words
on every key, including empty rows. This measures internal layout, not whole-verse ownership.
Empty untranslated verses count as exact no-ops and are separately disclosed above. A missing or
partial envelope, printed prose, or reference-only abbreviation is declined rather than forced.

The completed-round/cuts guards are excluded from inference match counts. Preservation is checked
separately: the guard retained every tested contiguous reviewed layout (966 Bodhi, 3670 Thanissaro),
and both plain full CLI runs reproduced ALL snapshot sutta/notes byte for byte, including omitted
verses and envelopes the measurement declines. Neither source data nor review answers were changed.
The previous report's regeneration drift is absent in this snapshot.
