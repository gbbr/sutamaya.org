Method: the review manifests define verses; actual sutta JSON rows supply the settled answers. All active findings were checked against those answers with zero conflicts. Exact means the same normalized words on every Pali key, including empty rows. Gold whole-verse ownership is supplied; internal cuts are withheld. This evaluates internal layout, not discovering whole-verse ownership. Untranslatable/reference-only/partial source envelopes are declined, not forced. Entirely untranslated verses count as exact no-ops and are separately disclosed.

The previous prototype uses lexical/length scoring over whole source-line cuts, with soft gap penalties. The refinement fixes equal counts at one whole source line per Pali row, occupies the first Pali row, forbids grouping source lines while another row could take them, and groups adjacent whole lines only when there are more source lines than rows. Speaker labels remain with speech. No verse IDs or review cuts enter independent inference.
